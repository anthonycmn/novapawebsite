## 25 words

Theatre and dance for ages 5–18 in Leesburg. Two-week summer camps ending in a
fully staged show, weekly classes, and one-day camps on school holidays.

## 50 words

Northern Virginia Performing Arts runs two-week Broadway Bound summer camps in
Leesburg where every camper is cast and every camper performs. Also weekly
acting, musical theatre, and dance classes, plus one-day camps on Loudoun,
Fairfax, and Prince William school holidays. Ages 5–18. Payment plans and
tuition assistance available.

## 100 words

Northern Virginia Performing Arts has spent more than a decade putting young
people on stage in Leesburg. Our Broadway Bound summer camps run two weeks,
Monday to Friday, 8:30 to 4:00, and finish with a fully staged production in
which every camper is cast and every camper performs. During the school year we
run weekly classes in acting, musical theatre, ballet, hip-hop, and improv for
ages 5 through adult, plus one-day camps on the days Loudoun, Fairfax, and
Prince William schools are closed. Staff are working theatre professionals,
background checked and trained to work with minors. Payment plans and
need-based tuition assistance are available.

## Targets

### Northern Virginia Magazine
- Runs an annual summer camp guide and family features.
- Contact via their editorial submissions page; ask for the camp guide deadline,
  which is usually set in **January or February** for the following summer.
- Send: 100-word block, three photos, dates, prices, ages, website.

### DC Theater Arts — DC-area theater summer camps guide
- Published annually, most recently in February.
- Editorial email listed on their site; they accept listings from area
  companies at no charge.
- Send: 50-word block plus dates, ages, prices, and a link.

### Others worth the same email
- **Fairfax Family Fun** — kid-friendly theatre directory
- **Loudoun County Parks & Rec** activity guide partner listings
- **Visit Loudoun** — things to do listings for the venue
- **Macaroni KID** Leesburg/Ashburn — camp fairs and guides
- **Northern Virginia Mag Best Of** — reader nomination window each spring
- **ActivityHero** marketplace — worth a listing that points back at
  our own registration system
