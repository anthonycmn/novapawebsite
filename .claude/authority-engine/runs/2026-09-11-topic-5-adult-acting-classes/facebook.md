## Post

She sat in her car in our parking lot for eleven minutes before her first
adult acting class. Forty-seven years old. Had emailed three times to ask if it
was really okay that she'd never done this before.

Ninety minutes later she was in the middle of the room, arms out, accusing a
total stranger of stealing her parking space, and the whole class was laughing
too hard for me to call time. She emailed from her driveway on the way home:
"I forgot I could do that."

That sentence is what an adult acting class is for. Not a career. Not
discovery. Just ninety minutes a week where you stop managing everybody's
opinion of you, including your own.

I wrote up the honest version for anyone who has been sitting in the parking
lot, so to speak: who actually takes these classes (almost never future
actors), whether you're too old (no, and I'll tell you why the question is a
trick), what happens minute by minute on the first night so there are no
surprises, the three fears every single beginner walks in with, and what a
class actually costs. Our Tuesday 7:30 adult class is $90 a month and the
first night is free, because I'd rather you find out in the room whether it's
right for you than on a credit card statement.

You are not too old. You are not too late. The door is unlocked.

Mr. CJ
