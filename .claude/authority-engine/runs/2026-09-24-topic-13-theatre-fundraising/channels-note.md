# Channel decisions — topic 13 (theatre fundraising)

**Facebook: skipped deliberately, not forgotten.**
NOVAPA25 is a parent page. This piece is written for theatre teachers and
program leaders — budget structure, administrator briefings, teacher burnout.
Posting it there puts "here is how programs make money off you" in front of the
families who buy the tickets, which is the wrong room for a good argument.

Where it actually travels: theatre-educator Facebook groups (EdTA chapters,
state thespian groups, the various "theatre teachers" groups). Those are
personal-account posts from CJ, not page posts, and they need a member who can
post without it reading as promotion. If you want a version tuned for a teacher
group, say so and I will write one — it is a different, shorter, more collegial
post than the page version would be.

**Reddit: skipped on pacing.**
r/Theatre last drafted Sept 18, six days ago, which is inside the one-post-per-
week-per-subreddit rule and too soon. r/MusicalTheatre was yesterday. r/nova is
excluded by standing instruction and would be wrong for this anyway.

This topic would land well on r/Theatre with the teacher audience, so it is
worth holding rather than forcing. Earliest sensible date: Sept 25 or later,
and it can ride with whatever the next teacher-lane topic is (19, 22, 23 and 26
are all in this lane) rather than needing its own slot.
