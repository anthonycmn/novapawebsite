If you've ever written a drama teacher an email that started with "I'm so sorry in advance," this one's for you.

Every season, Mr. CJ gets two of those emails the same week. One says "he has ADHD, I'm worried he'll be a disruption." The other says "she has anxiety, please tell me if I'm pushing her." Two families who had already decided their child was the problem. Neither was.

In this week's guide he walks through what the research actually shows about drama and anxious or inattentive kids (honestly, including where the evidence is thin), why one well-built rehearsal room works for both children at once, the job that turned a "disruption" into the kid running the whole show's entrances, what to look for when you visit a class, what to say to the teacher instead of apologizing, and when a class is not the right first step.
