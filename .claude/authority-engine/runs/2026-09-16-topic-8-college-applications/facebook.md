## Post

Every October a parent waits until the meeting is over and asks me the real question: "She loves it. But does theatre actually look good on college applications?"

The honest answer is yes, and not for the reason most families expect. Theatre isn't the credential. Theatre is the record: four years of showing up, growing into more responsibility, being depended on, and making something real in front of people who paid to be there. That reads the same on an application whether your kid is headed for nursing school, engineering, or a conservatory.

I wrote up the whole answer, including the part that matters most: how to actually describe those four years on the Common App so a reader can give credit for them. There's a version for the performer and a version for the stage manager, because both belong on that list, and a note for the senior wondering whether to drop theatre for one more AP.

Full guide is here: https://novapa.org/blog/does-theatre-look-good-on-college-applications-tony-cimino-johnson.html
