# Instagram — @cjtheatredirector — topic 12, unified auditions

## Caption

A ballroom at seven in the morning. Tables along three walls, one per school. A seventeen year old warming up in the stairwell, because the stairwell always has the best acoustics in the building.

I've set that room up. And every year, from the doorway, I hear a parent ask the same thing:

"So we have to go to Chicago in February?"

No. You probably don't. The word "unifieds" is doing three different jobs, and nobody ever explained that to you.

Here's the part I most want families to hear. In that room, your kid gets about five minutes. The faculty behind the table are not deciding whether your child is talented. They're making a match — is this person right for what this program does. A no is two lists failing to overlap. It is not a verdict.

New guide is up: what the three kinds of unifieds actually are, what the season costs line by line, and the rule for deciding whether to go at all. Link in bio.

— Mr. CJ
