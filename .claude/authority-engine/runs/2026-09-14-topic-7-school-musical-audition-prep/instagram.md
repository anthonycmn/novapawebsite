## Caption

I write the audition notice. Then I watch what families do with it.

Sixteen bars. Sheet music in your key. Callbacks by invitation. Eleven lines
on a half sheet, and every September they turn into two weeks of kitchen-table
drilling and a very tense car.

The part that does not fit on the half sheet: practice with a piano, not a
cappella. One messy run out of breath. One uncomfortable listener who is not
you. Then stop adding three days out.

And the line nobody explains: not getting a callback does not mean not getting
cast. A callback is a question I still have. Not getting one is often the
answer already being yes.

Full two-week plan, link in bio.

Mr. CJ

## Carousel (Canva: navy #08111F, gold #E8B84B, Playfair headline / DM Sans body)

**Slide 1 (cover):** "The audition notice, decoded." Small: "By the person who
writes it."

**Slide 2:** "'Sixteen bars'": A ceiling, not a target. A clean twelve beats a
rushed sixteen.

**Slide 3:** "'In the style of the show'": Not from the show. We have heard the
title song fourteen times by lunch.

**Slide 4:** "'Callbacks by invitation'": Not getting one does not mean not
getting cast. A callback is a question I still have.

**Slide 5:** "Two weeks out:" pick a song they can mean · get the cut and the key
settled · practice WITH a piano, not a cappella · 3 to 4 times a day, no more

**Slide 6:** "One week out:" the messy run (twenty jumping jacks, then sing) · one
uncomfortable listener who is not you · rehearse the walk-in

**Slide 7:** "Three days out: STOP ADDING." Small: "No new song. No key change. No
night-before run-through. That one is for the parent, and the kid can tell."

**Slide 8:** "What I am actually scoring:" can they take a note in the room · the
dance call, not the dancing · the hallway · the walk-in and the thank-you

**Slide 9 (close):** "They will not remember the sixteen bars. They will remember
whether the two weeks before felt like a test or like something they got to
do." Signed Mr. CJ. Link in bio.
