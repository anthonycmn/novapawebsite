2. Know which product you're buying: single-day camps | half-day hobby camps | full production camps. They are three different things at three different prices.
3. Ask the money question. If a camp won't publish its price, ask what the fee buys in HOURS ON TASK and what your child OPENS on the final weekend.
4. The five-question test: Is every child cast in a real show? Who is in the room? Is the price published? How are nervous first-timers handled? What happens on the last day?
