About the author. Tony Cimino-Johnson — my students call me Mr. CJ — is a Tony Awards–honored theatre educator, director, producer, and college audition coach with more than 20 years in theatre arts education. He is the author of The Theatre Leader's Playbook (Routledge) and the founder and CEO of Northern Virginia Performing Arts in Leesburg, Virginia. He writes about theatre leadership at CJ Creative on Substack.
