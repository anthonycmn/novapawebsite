House rules

Gold italics carry the emphasisOne italic gold phrase per heading, on the word that sells. Never two.
Never pure white body textBody is 88% white, secondary 62%. Pure #fff is reserved for headings.
Dark text never sits on darkEvery component defines its own text colors explicitly — no inherited surprises (the FAQ card bug, never again).
Counts are plain numbers"24 / 25" — no "left", no "filled", no words.
One CTA per viewThe gold gradient button appears once per screen. Everything else is the pill or a text link.
Playfair and Inter are bannedCormorant Garamond and DM Sans do their jobs. Georgia lives only inside HTML emails.
Emoji-free UICopy stays clean; icons are line SVGs in gold or ink.
Photography over decorationReal kids on real stages beats gradients. Overlay navy at 60-80% for text legibility.
