Meet the people
in the room.

You are handing us your child for an afternoon, a week, or a whole season. You should know who is going to be standing in front of them. Every person who teaches here is a working theatre professional and is background checked before their first day.

TCJ

Tony Cimino-Johnson

Chief Executive Officer · Director, Producer, Theatre Educator
MFA, Theatre Education MA, Drama Therapy · NYU 100+ productions

Tony has been directing and producing for over twenty years, with more than a hundred productions behind him. He has built arts organizations from nothing more than once, and he runs this one as CEO.

He holds an MFA in Theatre Education and a master’s in Drama Therapy from NYU, along with training in school violence prevention and bullying. Both degrees show up in how our rooms are run. Our staff learn how to talk to a nervous nine year old on a first day, and they learn that before they learn blocking.

His own directing takes young people into hard material. He staged 26 Pebbles about the aftermath of Sandy Hook, and The Trench and War Letters about war and loss. He has watched a room of teenagers change because of a scene they built themselves. That is why he keeps doing this.

“If you’re going to doubt something, doubt your limits.”

● Every adult in our building

Screened and trained before their first day

Every staff member completes a background check and mandatory training on working with minors before their first day. We strongly encourage CPR, First Aid, and AED certification and offer it onsite before employment begins, and we work to maintain age-appropriate supervision ratios whenever possible. Our Director of Health and Safety is a Licensed Clinical Social Worker, serving in a non-clinical safety and operations capacity; she does not provide clinical services, therapy, assessment, or diagnosis to NoVAPA students. The full detail is on our safety and staff training page.
