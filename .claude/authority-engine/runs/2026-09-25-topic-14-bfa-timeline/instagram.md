# Instagram — @cjtheatredirector — topic 14, BFA audition timeline

## Caption

"She decided in August that this is what she wants to do. Are we already too late?"

I get that email every September, and the answer is no. But here's the thing almost nobody tells families early enough.

The deadline that actually shapes your year isn't the audition. It's the prescreen — recorded songs, a monologue, often a dance reel — and most of those are due between November 1 and December 15.

The season everyone pictures, ballrooms in January and February, is downstream of a recording you make in October.

Nearly every family I've watched panic in January was panicking about the wrong month.

New guide is up: the whole year month by month, in two tracks — one for the student, one for the parent. And a section for anyone starting late, because that's the version nobody writes.

If you're behind: cut the number of schools before you cut the quality of anything.
