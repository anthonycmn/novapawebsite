# Instagram — @cjtheatredirector — topic 11, break camps

## Caption

The email always arrives in October, and it is never about theatre.

"Hi — are you open on November 3?"

That's the whole message. And for a while it stung a little, because I wanted the question to be about the work. Then I counted our calendar: between October and spring break, the divisions around us close for about eighteen weekdays. Nearly four working weeks where school is out and work is not.

So I stopped treating the childcare question as the lesser question.

A break camp has to answer two questions at once. Does this solve your Tuesday, and is your kid going to have a real day? Both, or neither.

And the thing I've watched happen over and over: the day you booked for the most practical reason in the world becomes the day they talk about at dinner all week.

New guide is up — what break camps cost, what to ask before you book, and when not to book one at all. Link in bio.

— Mr. CJ
