Fair questions, honest answers

My kid is shy.

So were half the kids who’ve stood on our stage. Nobody is pushed into a bigger part than they want. Some kids find their voice in the ensemble first. That counts.

How is this different from their shows and classes?

Camp is its own thing. One full day, its own piece, performed by 4:15. No lines to learn ahead, no costumes to track, nothing carries home. It fills the days the calendar leaves empty.

What if our schedule changes?

That’s the point of the punch card. Pick days now or as the school year unfolds. Credits are good through June 30, 2027 and live in your My NOVAPA account.

Is a full day long for a five year old?

It’s 8:30 to 4:15 broken into short bursts: games, music, movement, snack, stage. They’re far more likely to ask to stay late than to ask to leave early.

How do the snow days work?

When your school division closes for snow and we run a Snow Day Fun Day, a snow credit covers it. We decide early and post by 7 AM. Snow credits come free with packs booked by Labor Day.

Do dates fill?

Yes. Every camp day is capped by age group, and once a date fills, it’s filled. Credit holders come first on snow days.
