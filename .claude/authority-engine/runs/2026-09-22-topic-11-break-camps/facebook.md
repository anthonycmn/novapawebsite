# Facebook — NOVAPA25 page — topic 11, break camps

---

Every October we get the same email, and it is never about theatre.

"Hi — are you open on November 3?"

We know exactly what that means, and we want to say clearly: that is a completely good reason to book a camp day. School is closed and work is not. That's a Tuesday, and a room full of kids singing is a genuinely good place for your child to spend it.

So we wrote the honest guide we wish existed. What a break camp actually is, what one day can realistically deliver versus a full week, what these camps cost (with real numbers, ours and other programs'), seven questions worth asking any camp before you book, and — the part nobody writes — when NOT to book one.

It also has the 2027 dates in one place. Loudoun's spring break is March 22–26, winter break runs December 20 to January 3, and Thanksgiving break is November 22–28.

One thing from the guide worth saying out loud: compare the hours, not the tuition. A $450 week that runs 9:00 to 3:00 with paid aftercare can cost more than a longer day at a higher sticker price. Divide the price by hours in the building. That's your real number.

Read it here: https://novapa.org/blog/spring-break-theatre-camps-for-kids-tony-cimino-johnson.html
