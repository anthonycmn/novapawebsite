HOUSE RULES

Three fonts, one job eachProtest Strike headers. Cormorant italic whispers. DM Sans everything else. A fourth font is a bug.
One goldgold-3 solo for accents; a gradient only where the eye should land first. Solo gold-1/gold-2 are banned.
One gradient per viewThe gradient marks the single most important thing on screen. Two gradients = zero spotlights.
Cormorant never goes romanIf it's not italic, it's not Cormorant — it's a header (Protest) or content (DM Sans).
Never pure white body textBody 88% white, secondary 62%. Pure #fff belongs to headers.
Dark text never sits on darkEvery component declares its own colors — no inherited surprises.
Counts are plain numbers"24 / 25" — no words.
Emoji-free UI, photography over decorationLine icons in gold-3 or ink. Navy overlay 60-80% over photos for legibility.
