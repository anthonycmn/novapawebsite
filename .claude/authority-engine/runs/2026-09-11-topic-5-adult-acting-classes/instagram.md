## Caption

She sat in the parking lot for eleven minutes.

Forty-seven. Never acted. Emailed three times to ask if it was really okay.
Ninety minutes later she was mid-room, arms out, accusing a stranger of stealing
her parking space, and the class was laughing too hard for me to call time.

Her email from the driveway: "I forgot I could do that."

That is what an adult acting class is for. Not a career. Not discovery. Ninety
minutes a week where you stop managing everyone's opinion of you.

You are not too old. You are not too late. The door is unlocked.

minute on night one, and what it costs.
