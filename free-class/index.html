The short answers

What ages can participate?

Classes run in age groups: 5–8, 9–12, and 13–17, plus homeschool classes for ages 9–13. You pick the class that matches your child’s age when you book, so they walk into a room of kids their own age.

Does my child need any experience?

No. Most kids on our stage started with zero experience. The free day is built for first-timers — no audition, no preparation, nothing to memorize.

Do I have to enroll afterward?

No. The free day is yours with no obligation. If your child loves it, we’ll show you the season options. If not, we part as friends.

What does my child need to bring?

Comfortable clothes they can move in, sneakers, and a water bottle. Your confirmation email repeats all of this so you don’t have to remember it.
